### Report or deck

**You own the numbers. Short, sourced, and decidable.** For ads performance reports, business analyses, decks, and docs where the deliverable is not code. Lite tier unless the numbers drive money decisions.

1. **Name the reader and the decision.** Who reads it, and what they will decide from it. Keep exactly the scope asked for. When Andrew scopes out a brand or an account, its data stays out of the deliverable.
2. **Pull from the source.** Use the platform's API or connector, with the date range and account stated. No figure from memory, and no figure without its source.
3. **Explain the number** before you lead with it (pstack **principle-explain-the-number**). Name the attribution window, the currency, and what the metric counts. A spend jump or a cheaper cost per result needs its cause or the label "cause unknown".
4. **Reconcile the totals** against the platform's own summary for the same range. Label each figure as measured, derived, or estimated.
5. **Write it short.** Three to five findings, each with the action it suggests. Tables for comparisons. Apply **unslop**.
6. **Deliver in the format asked** (doc, slides, sheet) and link the file.

**Reply:** the link, the date range and sources, the findings in one line each, and any figure that did not reconcile.
